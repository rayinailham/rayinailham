# 00 — Product Overview

## Working Name
**Tempa** (placeholder, final TBD post-trademark check at DJKI Class 41 + 9 + 42).

User-preferred candidate "Edufield" rejected — descriptive mark, weak trademark, likely global collision.

## Tagline
**"Fight Your Way to PTN"**

## One-Line USP
Latihan UTBK dengan tutor AI personal + battlefield ranking sekolah kamu — mastery jujur, bukan grind kosong.

## Three Differentiators
1. **AI tutor RAG** — grounded in verified curated corpus, not generic chatbot
2. **School + bimbel + group leaderboard** — viral social moat no competitor has
3. **Honest mastery** — Elo-based skill tracker, not vanity grind count

## Target User (locked)
Grade 12 SMA reguler aspirant for **UTBK/SNBT 2027** prep cycle.

Primary archetype: serious tier-1 PTN aspirant (ITB / UI / UGM / IPB / ITS / Unair). Willing to pay 350-600rb. Heavy AI tutor user. Cares about leaderboard.

Secondary: middle-pack ambitious aspirant (will convert from free, follow streak, lower AI use).

Not target: low-effort browser, video-only learner, mass-market — Ruangguru can have them.

## Positioning Statement
"Untuk pelajar SMA kelas 12 yang serius UTBK, [brand] adalah platform latihan adaptif yang mengukur penguasaan materi dengan jujur — bukan menghitung jam menonton — agar kamu tahu persis mana yang harus dipelajari menuju PTN impian."

## vs Competitor
- **Ruangguru**: kami fokus latihan + AI, bukan video pasif
- **Zenius**: kami live data + adaptive, bukan content statis
- **Pahamify**: AI tutor kami pakai RAG terverifikasi, bukan chatbot generic
- **Bimbel offline**: 1/30 harga, fleksibel waktu, mastery terukur

## Brand Tone
**Tutor yang percaya dan dukung kamu — kayak trainer untuk warrior.** Selalu bantu bakar semangat, percaya kamu bisa, dorong waktu kamu lemah. Tegas tapi suportif. Tidak menggurui, tidak meremehkan. Bahasa Indonesia santai, occasional English tech term. Serius soal materi, hangat soal motivasi.

Tone signature:
- Believer: "Kamu bisa. Tinggal eksekusi."
- Trainer: "Mastery aljabar kamu turun 5%. Bangun, yuk fix sekarang — udah deket banget."
- Cheerleader (tegas): "3 hari skip? Bukan masalah. Hari ini kita bangun lagi."
- Battle-coach: "Soal ini berat — emang dirancang berat. Kamu udah lewatin yang lebih sulit."

Example copy good:
- "Streak kamu putus. Bangkit lagi. Hari ini soal pertama tunggu kamu."
- "Mastery Aljabar 67. Sedikit lagi Mahir. Push terus."
- "Gagal tryout? Bagus — sekarang kita tau gap kamu di mana. Let's fix."

Example copy avoid:
- "Hai sahabat belajarku! Yuk semangat ya~" (sappy)
- "Anda harus belajar lebih giat" (kaku, menggurui)
- "Tenang aja kok, gapapa kalau gagal" (lemah, tidak membakar)
- "Sebagai senior gue kasih tau..." (sok tahu, jarak)

Voice principle: **trainer di sudut ring, bukan dosen di podium.** Selalu di pihak user, tapi nggak ngalah waktu user butuh didorong.

## Visual Identity Direction
- **Mode**: dark mode primary (UTBK kid grind at night)
- **Palette**: deep navy + electric green/cyan accent (focus + progress) + warm orange highlight (achievement)
- **Typography**: Geist Sans / Inter for UI, serif for editorial (lesson reading)
- **Illustration**: minimal geometric, no cartoon mascot v1
- **Logo**: wordmark-first, monogram secondary

## Hero Metaphor
**Athlete training**, not soldier.

"Battlefield" feature internally war-coded OK. Overall brand = academic-serious + competitive-edge. Avoid full military aesthetic (alienate non-male, non-gamer). Sweet spot = athletic discipline.

## Three Pillars (the 3 features)
1. **Learning Section** — structured lesson + AI tutor sidebar + badge
2. **Battlefield** — adaptive practice + Elo + radar mastery
3. **Tryout Event** — scheduled exam + leaderboard + percentile

## Page Structure (locked)
Landing → Auth/Register → Dashboard → 3 Feature

## Narrative Hooks (3 for different funnel)

- **Viral angle**: "Sekolah kamu peringkat #berapa minggu ini? Ajak temenmu cek."
- **Anti-vanity angle**: "Berhenti hitung jam belajar. Mulai ukur penguasaan."
